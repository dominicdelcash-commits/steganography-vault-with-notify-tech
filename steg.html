Architecture Details
1. Telegram Intruder Dispatch: Silently sends an instant push notification with timestamp, carrier filename, and attempted password directly to your Telegram account.
2. In-Page Media Player: Autodetects MIME type and file extension. Renders native HTML5 <video controls>, <audio controls>, and <img> tags without third-party plugins.
